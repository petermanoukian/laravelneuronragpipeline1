# Unix Systems Performance Engineering and Security

### UNIX Performance Tuning and Storage Optimization

We specialize in deep system diagnostics and performance optimization for UNIX environments. Utilizing built-in profiling utilities such as `sar`, `vmstat`, `iostat`, `dtrace`, and `truss`, we pinpoint kernel lock contention, memory bottlenecks, and I/O latency across enterprise workloads.

Our team configures advanced storage layouts using ZFS on Solaris or JFS2 on AIX, tuning file system caching, buffer pools, and swap space for high-volume databases. We ensure your storage subsystems and memory managers operate at maximum efficiency under sustained heavy loads.

### Security Hardening, Auditing, and Disaster Recovery

We enforce strict security profiles on enterprise UNIX systems to meet regulatory standards like PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and CIS benchmarks. Our services include configuring Solaris RBAC, AIX Trusted Execution, auditing subsystem logs, and locking down network services to minimize attack vectors.

To protect against unplanned outages, we design robust disaster recovery configurations, including IBM HACMP/PowerHA, Solaris Cluster, and automated tape/network backup routines. We establish verified failover procedures and recovery strategies to guarantee business continuity for critical UNIX deployments.
