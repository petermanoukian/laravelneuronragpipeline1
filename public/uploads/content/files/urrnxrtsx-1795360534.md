# Unix Enterprise Architecture and Migration Solutions

### Enterprise UNIX Administration and Infrastructure Support

We provide comprehensive administration and maintenance for enterprise UNIX environments, including IBM AIX, Oracle Solaris, and HP-UX. Our team oversees multi-path storage integration, LVM management, and shell automation across complex hardware architectures such as IBM Power Systems and SPARC servers.

Our engineers ensure that legacy and mission-critical UNIX platforms remain operational, stable, and integrated with modern corporate environments. We handle patch management, user access governance, network interface bonding, and custom init script maintenance to maintain standard uptime across legacy infrastructure.

### Legacy UNIX Modernization and Cloud Migration Strategies

Transitioning business logic off legacy UNIX hardware requires careful planning and zero-downtime execution. We design structured migration roadmaps to transition workloads from Solaris, AIX, or HP-UX to modern Linux environments or cloud infrastructure without losing operational capability.

We analyze legacy C/C++ applications, custom Bourne/Korn shell scripts, and proprietary database configurations to ensure full compatibility during re-platforming. By re-architecting legacy UNIX services for modern hypervisors or container runtimes, we help organizations reduce hardware maintenance costs while preserving core business logic.
